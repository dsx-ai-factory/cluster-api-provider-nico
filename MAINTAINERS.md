## Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for:

- Reviewing and merging pull requests
- Triaging and responding to issues
- Cutting releases and maintaining the changelog
- Keeping CI green and dependencies up to date
- Setting the technical direction of the project

## Becoming a Maintainer

Consistent contributors who have demonstrated good judgment and a deep
understanding of the codebase may be nominated for maintainership. Open an
issue proposing the change and reach consensus among the existing maintainers.
